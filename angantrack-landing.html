Key Features

Everything Anganwadi Workers Need

Designed for real field conditions — low connectivity, limited training, maximum impact.

QR-Based Child ID

Each child gets a unique QR card. Workers scan in seconds — no typing, no errors, instant profile load.

Auto Weight Measurement

HX711 load cell captures weight automatically. No manual entry, no human measurement error.

WHO Z-Score AI

AI engine calculates WHO-standard Z-scores in real time and flags malnutrition risk levels automatically.

Height Tracking

Ultrasonic HC-SR04 sensor measures standing height. Growth charts generated automatically over time.

Parent Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging sends instant alerts to parents' Android app — in Tamil and local languages.

Offline-First Storage

All data stored locally on ESP32. Batch syncs to cloud when connectivity resumes — no data ever lost.

Workflow

How It Works — 5 Steps

From QR scan to parent notification in under 15 seconds, even without internet.

1

Scan Child QR Card

Worker scans the child's unique QR code. Profile, previous measurements, and health history load instantly — even offline.

2

Automatic Weight Capture

Child stands on the kiosk platform. The HX711 load cell captures precise weight with 24-bit accuracy — no manual input.

3

Height Measurement

HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor calculates standing height in real time. Combined with weight for BMI and Z-score.

4

AI Analysis & Risk Flag

Django backend computes WHO Z-scores. Red/yellow/green status assigned. Malnutrition alerts generated automatically.

5

Parent Notified + Cloud Sync

Firebase pushes alert to parent's app instantly. When internet is available, all offline records sync to the dashboard.

Technical Specifications

Microcontroller — ESP32 Dual-core 240 MHz, WiFi + Bluetooth, 4MB flash
Weight Sensor — HX711 + Load Cell 24-bit ADC, ±0.1g precision, 0–150kg range
Height Sensor — HC-SR04 Ultrasonic, 2cm–400cm range, ±3mm accuracy
QR Reader Module Embedded camera with OpenCV QR decode
